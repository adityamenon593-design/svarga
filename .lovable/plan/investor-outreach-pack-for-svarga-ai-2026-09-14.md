# Investor Outreach Pack for Svarga.ai

A one-time research + writing deliverable. No app code changes.

## Note on WhatsApp numbers

Investors' personal WhatsApp numbers are not public, and sourcing them would be a privacy problem (and usually backfires — cold WhatsApp to a partner reads as spam). What actually works: official pitch forms, partner email, and LinkedIn/X DMs. Every message below is written so you can paste it into WhatsApp unchanged the moment you get a number through a warm intro.

## What gets created

A single file, `docs/investor-outreach.md`, with four sections.

### 1. Ranked shortlist (all four types you asked for)

Roughly 12 targets, each with: why they fit Svarga specifically, what they typically write at your stage, and their official public contact route (pitch form URL, partner email where published, LinkedIn/X handle).

- Indian VCs: Peak XV Surge, Blume Ventures, Accel India (Atoms), Elevation Capital
- AI-focused funds: Together Fund, Neon Fund, Lightspeed India AI practice
- Angels / dharma-aligned: Kunal Shah, Nithin/Nikhil Kamath (Gruhas), spiritual-tech operator angels
- Strategic: Jio Platforms, Zoho, Krutrim, Tata Digital

Every claim about programme names and contact routes gets verified by web search before it goes in the file — anything I can't confirm is marked unverified rather than stated.

### 2. Recommended ask

Pre-seed, ₹40L–₹1Cr on a SAFE, framed as 12–15 months of runway to reach paying-user traction. Reasoning: Svarga is live with payments, quotas and a distinct Krishna/Vedic product wedge, but revenue is still early. Accelerator programmes (Surge, Atoms) are the highest-probability first door at this stage.

### 3. Outreach messages

Four ready-to-send drafts, each under 150 words, no hype, founder voice:

- Cold email to a VC partner
- LinkedIn connection note + follow-up
- WhatsApp version (short, for warm intros)
- Strategic/corporate partnership variant

All include: Made in India, 39 languages, Baby Krishna buddy, live product at svarga.digital, your BCA background, adityamenon593@gmail.com.

### 4. What to have ready before they reply

A 10-line traction snapshot to fill in (users, paid conversions, retention), plus the three questions every investor will ask first and honest one-line answers.
