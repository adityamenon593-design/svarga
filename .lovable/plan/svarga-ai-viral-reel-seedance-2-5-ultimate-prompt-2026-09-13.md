## Honest notes (kept real)

- Video models garble long text — on-screen words in the prompt are kept short for that reason; if any text renders wrong, the fix is a re-roll or trimming to fewer on-screen words, not a longer prompt.
- "39 languages" is your claim — the reel shows ~10 scripts visually and states 39 in text/caption.
- One clip cannot guarantee virality; the caption, posting time (evening IST), and you commenting/sharing from your network matter as much as the video.
