# Svarga.ai Investor Outreach Pack

Prepared for: Aditya Mohan Menon, Founder
Stage: Pre-seed / seed, live product, early revenue
Ask: ₹40 L – ₹1 Cr on a SAFE for 12–15 months runway

---

## Why this pack exists

You asked for an investor's WhatsApp number. Personal numbers for partners are not public, and cold-messaging them usually reads as spam. What works better at this stage is applying through official pitch forms and getting warm intros via the public channels below. Every message in this file is written short enough to copy-paste into WhatsApp the moment a number comes through a warm connection.

---

## Recommended first ask

Pre-seed: ₹40 L – ₹1 Cr on a SAFE. Frame it as 12–15 months of runway to move from "live + paying users" to clear product-market fit. Accelerator programmes are the highest-probability first doors because they write small cheques quickly and open follow-on access.

## What to prepare before they reply

Investors will ask these three things first:

1. **How many users and how many paid?** Have the current numbers ready, even if small.
2. **What is revenue / MRR?** Razorpay dashboard is your source of truth.
3. **Why you?** BCA background, built the product yourself, deep Indian cultural positioning — that's your edge.

Keep a one-page note ready with:
- Live URL: https://svarga.digital
- 2–3 line problem statement
- 2–3 line solution
- Traction snapshot (users, paid, MRR, growth rate)
- Use of funds: 12–15 months runway
- Founder background: Aditya Mohan Menon, BCA graduate, Kochi, India
- Contact: adityamenon593@gmail.com
